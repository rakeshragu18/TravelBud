# TravelBud - MVP Success Metrics

## 1. Objective

Measure whether TravelBud helps users plan trips more easily, generate useful itineraries and understand travel recommendations.

## 2. Key Metrics

| Metric | What it measures | How to measure |
|---|---|---|
| Itinerary Completion Rate | Users who successfully generate an itinerary | Successful itinerary generations ÷ total planning attempts × 100 |
| Time to Generate Itinerary | Time taken to receive a usable itinerary | Average time from submitting trip details to displaying the itinerary |
| Itinerary Usefulness | How helpful users find the generated plan | Post-task user rating |
| Planning Task Success Rate | Users who complete the trip-planning task | Successful task completions ÷ total attempts × 100 |
| Price Clarity | Whether users understand estimated versus confirmed prices | Percentage of users who correctly identify price status |
| User Satisfaction (CSAT) | Overall satisfaction with the planning experience | Short satisfaction survey after using the planner |
| Error Rate | Frequency of failed planning requests | Failed requests ÷ total requests × 100 |

## 3. Initial Validation Plan

Test the TravelBud prototype with a small group of target users.

1. Ask users to enter their trip details.
2. Observe whether they can generate and understand an itinerary.
3. Record completion time and any errors.
4. Ask users to rate the usefulness of the itinerary.
5. Collect feedback on confusing steps and missing information.
6. Prioritize improvements based on user feedback and observed issues.

## 4. Proposed Initial Targets

The following are suggested targets for initial testing, not measured results or validated benchmarks.

- Itinerary Completion Rate: At least 80%
- Planning Task Success Rate: At least 80%
- Average Itinerary Usefulness: At least 4 out of 5
- User Satisfaction: At least 4 out of 5
- Error Rate: Below 10%

Review these targets after collecting baseline results from prototype testing.

## 5. Measurement Considerations

- Define a planning attempt consistently before calculating completion and error rates.
- Separate technical failures from users abandoning the process.
- Test with different destinations, budgets, and trip durations.
- Treat AI-generated prices and recommendations as estimates unless verified by reliable live data.
- Track actual results before claiming that the MVP has met its targets.

## 6. Next Steps

Use prototype testing to establish baseline performance, identify usability issues and refine the MVP. Revisit the metrics and targets as the product evolves.
