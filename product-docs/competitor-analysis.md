# TravelBud --- Competitor Analysis

**Project:** TravelBud - AI Travel Assistant\
**Purpose:** Product Management capstone\
**Positioning:** Trust-first, AI-powered travel Assistant\
**Tagline:** Plan Smarter, Travel Better

## 1. Executive Summary

TravelBud was conceptualized to address recurring friction in the online
travel-planning journey: fragmented planning across platforms, unclear
or changing prices, booking-management difficulties, refund uncertainty,
unreliable information and customer-support challenges.

The capstone research considered Expedia, Booking.com and Priceline as
key competitors. The wider project also references MakeMyTrip and
Goibibo, and includes a hands-on purchase-flow comparison of Expedia and
MakeMyTrip for a round-trip flight search from Bengaluru.

The product opportunity identified for TravelBud is to combine trip
research, flight and hotel comparison, AI-assisted itinerary planning,
budget support, booking-management concepts, refund visibility and
customer assistance in a more unified and trust-focused experience.

> **Evidence note:** This document summarizes the existing capstone
> materials. Review themes are presented as qualitative findings from
> the project research, not as independently verified rankings or
> statistically representative market results. The source materials
> describe collecting 10 reviews per platform across review sources
> including Google Play Store, ProductReview, ConsumerAffairs, and
> Trustpilot. The detailed hands-on checkout experiment in the project
> compares Expedia with MakeMyTrip, not all three primary competitors.

## 4. Competitor Review

### Expedia

**Role in the capstone:** One of the primary competitors and a subject
of the hands-on checkout-flow experiment.

**What the project observed in the tested flow** - The round-trip flight
purchase attempt took approximately **4 minutes 8 seconds**. - The flow
was recorded as search → flight selection → fare details → passenger
details → payment. - The experiment reached the payment stage. -
Friction points included search-result loading time, fare rules hidden
in expandable sections, hotel or package recommendations interrupting
the flow, and limited progress indication. - The project rated
navigation, booking speed, information clarity and checkout simplicity
more favourably than in the tested MakeMyTrip flow.

**Implications for TravelBud** - Keep the primary planning journey
focused and easy to follow. - Make fare conditions and total-cost
information easy to find. - Avoid distracting recommendations before
users complete their main task.

**Evidence boundary:** These observations come from one documented
purchase-flow experiment. They should not be generalized to every
Expedia user or every booking type.

### Booking.com

**Role in the capstone:** One of the three primary competitors
considered in the review research and market analysis.

The project places Booking.com in the broader online travel-platform
landscape and identifies customer needs around trustworthy accommodation
information, pricing transparency, booking flexibility and reliable
support.

**Implications for TravelBud** - Make accommodation comparisons and
relevant trust information easy to evaluate. - Help users understand the
relationship between accommodation choices, trip plans and budget. -
Treat trust, transparent terms and post-booking support as part of the
end-to-end journey.

**Evidence boundary:** The source materials available for this document
do not provide a separate, quantified Booking.com checkout experiment or
a validated platform-specific scorecard. The implications above are
product opportunities derived from the capstone's overall findings, not
claims that every listed weakness is unique to Booking.com.

### Priceline

**Role in the capstone:** One of the three primary competitors
considered in the review research and market analysis.

The capstone's overall findings highlight pricing transparency,
comparison, reliable booking information, changes and cancellations and
customer support as important customer needs across online travel
platforms.

**Implications for TravelBud** - Help users compare options using clear
total-cost information. - Clearly explain assumptions, restrictions, and
whether a price is an estimate or verified live data. - Connect trip
planning with itinerary management and support instead of treating the
journey as only a transaction.

**Evidence boundary:** The project materials used for this analysis do
not contain a separate, measured Priceline checkout experiment or enough
platform-specific evidence to assign it a comparative score. The
opportunities listed here are based on the shared customer problems
identified in the capstone.

## 6. Cross-Competitor Synthesis

The strongest cross-platform themes recorded in the capstone are:

1.  **Trust:** Users need confidence in listings, booking reliability
    and the information used to make decisions.
2.  **Transparency:** Pricing, fees, fare rules, cancellation
    conditions and estimates should be clear.
3.  **Journey continuity:** Research, comparison, itinerary planning
    and post-booking support are often experienced as separate tasks.
4.  **Post-booking resolution:** Changes, cancellations, refunds and
    support are important beyond the initial purchase.
5.  **Reduced friction:** Unnecessary decision points, confusing
    options and interruptions can increase effort and undermine
    confidence.
6.  **Personalized assistance:** Travellers want recommendations that
    reflect their destination, dates, budget, travel style and
    preferences.

The project research suggests that customers value **trust,
transparency and reliability---not simply more features**.


## 7. Conclusion

The capstone's competitor and customer research points to an opportunity
for TravelBud to differentiate through a more unified, transparent and
trust-focused travel-planning experience. Rather than competing only on
the number of listings or booking options, TravelBud aims to support
travellers across research, itinerary planning, budget decisions, and
travel assistance.

The product's central hypothesis is that reducing fragmentation and
uncertainty can make trip planning easier and help travellers make more
confident decisions. This hypothesis should be tested with users and
validated through reliable data integrations before TravelBud expands
into live booking, refund tracking or automated booking actions.
